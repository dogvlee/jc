Remote open-source Chinese fonts
================================

The editor loads the following font families on demand. Font binaries are not
bundled in this web application. Each family is supplied under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1 (OFL-1.1):

Noto Sans SC; Noto Serif SC; Ma Shan Zheng; ZCOOL XiaoWei;
ZCOOL QingKe HuangYou; ZCOOL KuaiLe; Long Cang; Zhi Mang Xing;
Liu Jian Mao Cao; WDXL Lubrifont SC; Noto Sans TC; Noto Serif TC;
LXGW WenKai TC; LXGW WenKai Mono TC; LXGW Marker Gothic; Huninn;
Iansui; Chiron GoRound TC; Chocolate Classical Sans;
Cactus Classical Serif.

Authoritative source and license records:
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl

Exact-version webfont delivery:
https://fontsource.org/docs/getting-started/cdn
